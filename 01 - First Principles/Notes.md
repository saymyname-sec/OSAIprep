# Module 1 — Introduction to Red Teaming AI Systems

## Overview
This module introduces the discipline of offensive security applied to AI systems. It frames AI red teaming as a distinct practice from traditional red teaming, covering the unique attack surfaces, threat actor mindsets, and regulatory context that practitioners must understand before engaging AI-enabled targets.
